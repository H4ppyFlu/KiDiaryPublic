# The diary is child-centric

Kidiary is a record of the child, not a record of the parents' experience of parenting — an archive that could plausibly be handed to the child when they are grown. The prompt bank may still include parent-facing questions ("what was hard today?"), because those are often what makes someone answer at all on a bad day, but the archive's subject is the child.

## Consequences

The child is a first-class entity with a name and a birthdate rather than an implicit constant, so that every answer can be displayed as "at 2 years, 4 months" — the highest-value derived field in an archive meant to be read years later, and one that cannot be reconstructed if the birthdate is never captured.

This also leaves room for a second child without reshaping the schema, though only one is modelled in practice today.
